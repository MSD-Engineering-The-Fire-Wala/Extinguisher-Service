Client questions

Frequently asked questions

Every brand has to meet different safety testing standards. Certified (ISI / approved) extinguishers are manufactured and pressure-tested to defined standards, while uncertified cylinders may not have gone through the same testing. Certified products offer more reliability, a longer service life and are easier to justify during inspections.
the quantity and type of extinguishers are calculated based on the area, height, and occupancy of your premises. Applicable standards (such as NBC and IS 2190) and local fire authority requirements decide the exact number — our team can survey your site and recommend it. If the area is large, one extinguisher may not be sufficient to provide adequate coverage and protection.
We follow the complete refilling process — pre-weighing, emptying, internal cleaning, MAP powder refilling, nitrogen re-pressurization, or leakage testing. We check every cylinder’s pressure gauge reading and verify its weight. We do not simply apply a new sticker. At The Fire Wala, you receive a refill certificate, tag, and photographic proof along with the refilling service. We follow the maintenance practices of IS 2190.
Cheap refilling may save money in the short term, but it can lead to long-term risks. Low-grade powder or improper charging can lead to nozzle blockage and pressure loss. We use quality extinguishing agent and nitrogen pressure charging, and check pressure and leakage before handover. And now our refilling is just ₹100/kg + GST (limited period offer).
After refilling, we provide you with a Refilling Certificate, Test Report, and Sticker (Date & Next Due). We maintain photographic proof of every cylinder. These records help you show your maintenance history during inspections and audits. We believe in transparency and accountability.
We pressure-test and leak-test every extinguisher and attach a ‘Ready for Use’ tag. However, if timely refilling is not carried out, the pressure naturally drops, increasing the risk of failure during a fire. Timely refilling means a working system.
Old cylinders need periodic hydrostatic testing, and the chances of corrosion or fatigue increase with age. Inspecting authorities may object to old or damaged cylinders. Our team will tell you honestly whether refilling or replacement is the right choice. Buying a new system ensures safety and compliance.

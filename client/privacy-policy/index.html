Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MSD Engineering – The Fire Wala ("we", "us", "our"), a fire safety services business based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, collects and uses personal information when you visit our websites, contact us, or fill in a form on our website or in our ads on Facebook, Instagram or Google.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Email address (if you provide it)
  • Company or organisation name, and your role
  • Site location or area
  • Details of your requirement, for example the number, type and size of fire extinguishers, or the service you need
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, phone or email

When you visit our website, basic technical information (such as pages visited, device type and approximate location) may be collected through cookies and analytics tools, for example Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. When you interact with our ads, Meta (Facebook / Instagram) and Google may share information about the ad response with us according to their own policies.

2. How we use your information

  • To call or message you about your enquiry and share a quotation
  • To schedule pickup, refilling, delivery, inspection or other services you request
  • To send service reminders, such as your next refilling due date, if you are our customer
  • To keep records of the services we provide
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are useful, and to improve them

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

Your information is used by our own team. It is stored and processed using trusted service providers, such as Google (Workspace, Sheets, Ads), Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) and our website hosting provider, only to run our business. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records only as long as needed for the purposes above, for providing service reminders, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see section 6).

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Access is limited to people in our team who need it to serve you. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

6. Your choices and rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Delete your information, or stop contacting you
  • Withdraw consent you gave earlier

To make a request or raise a concern, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tools for basic analytics and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date.
